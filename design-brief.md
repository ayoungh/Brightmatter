# Brightmatter AI Design Brief

## Design read

Brightmatter is for teams adopting AI without wanting their work to feel colder. The page should feel optimistic, exact, editorial, and distinctly human.

## Concept spine

The site is a field instrument: it brings scattered signals into focus and turns them into a clear next move.

## Delivery tier

Editorial cinema. Motion is present and tactile but never delays the content. The reference treatment explicitly opts out of a full-site video journey.

## Identity system

- Theme paradigm: Pristine Light with decisive bright colour fields.
- Background character: solid fields with quiet atmospheric depth.
- Typography character: editorial serif with rational sans.
- Hero architecture: editorial offset with an image-as-canvas.
- Section system: alternating editorial blocks with Swiss-grid discipline.
- Signature components: layered image crop frames, vertical rhythm lines, hover-accordion slices, product UI panel stack.
- Narrative spine: tool and precision instrument.
- Second-read moment: one oversized circular aperture used as the hero grade-shift mask.

## Locked palette

- Deep ink: `#1D2E35`
- Solar yellow: `#F6F27C`
- Ice: `#DFF5FA`
- Soft white: `#F8FAF5`
- Action blue: `#147C9F`

Solar and ice create an optimistic daylight world. Action blue is the only interactive accent and passes AA with white text.

## Locked typography

Newsreader is the display face because the brand must balance machine intelligence with human judgment. IBM Plex Sans carries body copy, controls, and technical labels.

## Tier-1 interaction

B2 Grade-shift interaction pair. Two aligned renders of the same hero scene move from calm observation to illuminated analysis through a pointer and scroll-controlled feathered aperture. The interaction enacts the field-instrument concept by bringing the useful signal into focus.

## Motion rules

- Use Motion for transform, clip-path, mask position, and height only.
- All content is complete on first paint.
- Reduced motion shows the bright final hero and static section compositions.
- No generated video, infinite decorative loops, or viewport-gated opacity reveals.

## Section plan

1. Navigation and asymmetric hero: editorial offset.
2. Product journey: sticky media with four ruled chapters.
3. Capability explorer: full-width interactive slices.
4. Connected context: dark diptych with generated product composition.
5. Audience use cases: horizontal editorial rail.
6. Trust principles: centered statement with four columns.
7. FAQ: ruled accordion.
8. Conversion and footer: solar field with deep-ink base.

Eyebrow budget: three across eight sections.

## Asset plan

- One horizontal reference board per section.
- Two aligned hero grades.
- Four workflow photographs.
- One generated product-interface composition.
- Two atmospheric surface plates.
- One coherent six-icon sheet.
- Brightmatter monogram and favicon source.
- One 1200 by 630 social card.

All generated imagery is original, contains no embedded typography, logos, or watermarks, and uses the locked palette.

## CTA inventory

- Navigation: compact corner-bracket target, label `See the workflow`.
- Hero: rounded route capsule with travelling arrow, label `See the workflow`.
- Capability explorer: full-row target that shifts its image crop, label `Explore capability`.
- Footer: oversized circular aperture target, label `See the workflow`.

## Responsive behavior

- Hero copy precedes media on mobile.
- Sticky desktop media becomes normal-flow media cards on mobile.
- Capability slices become vertically stacked disclosure rows.
- Audience rail becomes horizontal snap scrolling.
- The conversion capsule docks above the mobile safe area and never covers focused elements or the footer.

## Anti-convergence ledger

This is the first build in the chat. Its six axes are solar and ice palette, Newsreader plus IBM Plex Sans, editorial image-as-canvas hero, B2 grade shift, route and aperture CTA garments, and consistently soft sixteen to thirty-two pixel corners.
